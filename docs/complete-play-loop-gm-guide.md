# Complete Play Loop GM guide

## Guided item adjudication

Guided requests are private GM work backed by reviewed structured definitions. Open the Campaign queue, verify the actor, safe source label, timing, explicit reusable/consumable disposition, and bounded choices, then accept or cancel.

For interpretive campaign tools the only generic choice is **Accept reviewed use**. Acceptance records reviewed use and exact source disposition; it does not invent a recipe, output, target, modifier, condition, or battlefield mutation. Consumables reserve one unit and consume only on acceptance. Cancellation releases the reservation. Reusable tools remain in exact custody. Durable private receipts support replay and audit.

If source custody, definition, actor, revision, or a choice changed, reload. Do not expand the request from prose or make a private sheet edit to approximate it.

## Finish Encounter

Open **Finish Encounter** from the GM Director. The review sheet blocks commitment until every current provider is complete:

1. eligibility and unresolved gates;
2. persistent consequences;
3. reward package;
4. Experience allocations;
5. loot and money allocations;
6. capture destinations and naming;
7. outcomes;
8. temporary cleanup.

Review the exact current preview, check confirmation, and commit once. One transaction applies rewards, captures, sheet/group writes, cleanup, immutable history facts, Campaign attention seeds, encounter/map completion, and realtime events. A player cannot see the Director control or GM/private provider evidence.

A timeout keeps the exact commit command. Use status recovery before any retry. Do not create another settlement draft or manually apply one domain while status is unknown.

## Correction

Accepted settlement evidence is immutable. Use the GM correction workflow to append one current, hash-bound correction authority. Correction must preserve the original accepted document and facts, explain the safe result, revalidate current revisions, and commit audit plus realtime together.

Players cannot correct. Stale authority, payload drift, a competing correction, or a partial failure changes nothing. Never correct by rewriting a receipt, history fact, operation row, or accepted settlement JSON.

## Campaign attention

Campaign attention is a current role-scoped snapshot, not a to-do cache. Items point to exact current authority and immutable source events while exposing only safe labels and routes.

Review blocking work first:

- Pokémon Move, Ability, Evolution, and form choices;
- Trainer Features, Edges, Skills, and milestone alternatives;
- recovery, treatment, and multi-day resources;
- capture, hatch, naming, roster overflow, ownership, and Profile linkage;
- incompatible equipment;
- retained-operation recovery.

Destination workflows enumerate current legal choices. Do not resolve an attention item by deleting it or editing a note. It closes only when its owning authority proves completion or no-longer-applicable state.

## Next day and next scene

**Next day** is intentionally secondary. Run preflight, inspect blockers and bounded sheet impact, confirm, then commit that exact reviewed authority. Preflight is a rolled-back production dry run; it does not publish or persist recovery.

Active encounters, unfinished settlements, and blocking attention prevent advancement. Commit rechecks campaign clock, sheets, maps, Eggs, effects, and attention inside the transaction. After acceptance, Campaign reloads the whole continuation snapshot. Start the next scene only from those fresh routes and authorities.

## GM troubleshooting

- **Action unavailable:** read the adjacent reason; reload current offers instead of bypassing it.
- **Every unit reserved:** recover or cancel the owning pending request.
- **Definition changed:** discard only after exact status confirms no acceptance, then redeclare.
- **Settlement stale:** reopen Finish Encounter and review all providers again.
- **Unknown commit result:** check status; never click around to recreate rewards.
- **Attention remains:** open its route and verify the underlying current authority; presentation text alone cannot resolve it.
