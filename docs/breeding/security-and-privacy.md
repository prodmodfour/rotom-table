# Breeding security, consent, privacy, and abuse policy

The executable policy inventory is `data/breeding-automation/security-policy.json` (`breeding-security-privacy-v1`). This document explains the reviewed boundary; server contracts and tests must remain hash-bound to the JSON definition.

## Trust boundary

Breeding is a campaign operation. A browser may request a preview, choose a server-issued option, grant narrowly scoped consent, or retry the same selector intent after reconnect. It may not submit an operation command, species resolution, parent roles, traits, inheritance, elapsed time, a child patch, rewards, provider effects, or authority claims.

The server owns:

- campaign and profile authorization;
- Trainer control and parent linkage;
- current parent revisions and effective providers;
- consent validity;
- compiled species and family facts;
- checks, rolls, choices, spends, and time;
- project, Egg, lineage, child, and acquisition persistence;
- role-specific projections and realtime access.

Unknown fields, identities, audiences, mechanics, revisions, or contradictory results fail closed.

### Breeder Edge information flow

The `Breeder` handoff is server-private authority. The server reloads the current Trainer and campaign clock, resolves Edge effectiveness and the current mandated Skill itself, and binds the result to current Profile control or exact authenticated GM verification. Direct acquisition uses Pokémon Education and requires Novice. A `Dilettante` grant is reconstructed through the current effective Feature projection, verifies the exact Feature-to-Edge source, waives the Skill prerequisite, and accepts only a server-owned command-bound choice of General Education or Perception for the mandated rank/check application. The Feature contribution, source instance, selected Skill, rank, and total are folded into the effective evidence hash. A client cannot provide any projection, source/instance identity, Skill choice/rank/total, hash, dependency, contribution, or delegated outcome. The restricted bundle is accepted only by server mechanics and must never be projected, logged as a normal response, placed in local storage, or embedded in realtime events.

Edge automation may establish only effective permission and the reviewed `breeding-project-request` / `breeder-dc12-timeline` contribution identities. It cannot calculate Project progress, authorize consent, roll the d20, decide DC 12 success, create an Egg, or mutate lineage. Duplicate, unresolved, suppressed, stale, malformed, asynchronous, or source-drifted authority fails without mutation. Direct below-Novice authority fails; Dilettante authority fails without exact Feature evidence and a closed synchronous substitution. Campaign-shared Breeder authority remains closed.

### Feature provider information flow

The BR-061 Feature handoff is also server-private. It reconstructs the current effective, unsuppressed, parameter-complete Feature set from the stored Trainer, binds canonical record/runtime hashes and current campaign time, and emits only typed modifier-inventory values plus matching read-set attestations. A generic adapter may turn those values into self-hashed BR-046 provider snapshots. This is evidence, not client-visible mechanics or mutation authority: Feature automation does not gain ownership of Projects, Eggs, randomness, incubation, hatching, child construction, lineage, or outcomes.

`Playing God` evidence is bounded to one canonical reviewed Species, fixed Level 5 and one-day parameters, choice flags, and current Technology Education rank bounds. The handoff alone still proves neither currency/tool custody nor execution. Only the BR-067 artificial-Egg use case may rebuild one exact Chemistry Set row, deduct $3500, persist required rolls, consume rank-bound offers, and insert the shared Egg atomically; the tool remains in custody. Hatch-special Feature evidence cannot force an outcome without exact persisted rank-bounded use consumption. Fossil Restoration and Prehistoric Bond execute only inside the BR-065 fossil reducer after current source, Edge, Skill, tool, and GM authority succeed; learning providers remain gated to BR-068. Suppression, missing parameters, unresolved identities, stale access, source/runtime drift, forged values, provider exceptions, and Promise-like results all fail without writes. The canonical facility registry is empty; a nonempty or free-text facility claim cannot confer authority or become a campaign-inventory oracle.

## Consent

Cross-owner breeding requires positive consent tied to one project, one parent slug and revision, one consenting profile, bounded scopes, campaign-time expiry/revocation state, an operation ID, and a command hash. It is not a reusable blanket grant.

A grant is accepted only when the profile currently controls the parent owner Trainer and that Trainer currently links the parent. Production rechecks control, linkage, parent revision, expiry, and revocation in the authoritative transaction. Expiry is effective at exact campaign-minute equality. Revocation requires that same consenting Profile's current owner-Trainer control and settles the immutable grant once; GM recovery authority cannot stand in for positive consent. Revocation after Egg acceptance preserves the audit but cannot rewrite the Project, Egg, parent snapshots, or offspring blueprint.

The following never count as consent:

- selecting a parent in a browser;
- a public or player-visible sheet flag;
- a legacy session grant;
- prior participation in another project;
- a free-form note;
- another participant's claim.

A parent revision change before the snapshot invalidates consent and returns the project to awaiting consent. Revocation before Egg acceptance blocks production and remains audited. Revocation after acceptance does not rewrite the immutable Egg or parent snapshot. A GM override requires a typed reason ID and audited operation.

## Audience projections

There are five separate audiences, not one payload hidden with CSS:

- **Public** receives only a configured bounded summary and coarse public-safe lifecycle status.
- **Owner** receives their authorized project or Egg workflow, exact progress, safe explanations, choices they are allowed to make, and resolved offspring facts allowed by owner policy.
- **Participating owner** receives the consent request, their own parent's safe summary, and only their own contribution attribution. They do not receive the other parent's hidden identity or sheet.
- **GM** receives full mechanics, parents, consent, rolls, overrides, lineage, recovery, and audit state.
- **Diagnostic operator** receives hashes and traces only, never sheet, command, option, note, or private profile payloads.

Inheritance candidates shown to the Egg owner omit private attribution to another owner's parent. Full source attribution is GM-only; a participant may see attribution for their own parent.

Incubation progress has a separate owner/GM projection. It exposes only Egg identity/revision, coarse lifecycle status, target/accumulated/remaining campaign minutes, basis points, pause/readiness state, durable clock checkpoints, and authorized actions. It omits offspring traits, parents, Breeder, Profiles, consent, provider evidence, rolls, offers, commands, receipts, and hashes. Participating-owner access is not an Egg audience and must fail before projection.

Manual readiness is GM-only and requires a closed reason plus one self-targeted command-bound recovery override. Its restricted GM projection may include the typed correction reason, exact correction minute, accepted/current Egg revisions, and progress totals. The owner projection may disclose only the coarse `gm-mark-ready` kind; it must not disclose the reason, override, command, read set, receipt, policy evidence, or hashes. `mark-egg-ready` is never projected as an owner action and is absent for paused or non-incubating Eggs. Realtime remains refresh-only for diagnostic, GM, owner, and public audiences and structurally excludes participating owners.

Campaign-clock Egg batching is also GM-only. Server discovery—not a client list—owns the canonical bounded due page. Its projection may reveal Egg and operation IDs, coarse child status, clock checkpoint, revision transition, credited/skipped/overflow totals, readiness crossing, and whether another page is due. It must omit blueprints, species and traits, parents, Breeder, Profiles, consent, provider payloads, commands, read sets, receipts, and hashes. Each child uses the ordinary owner-scoped Egg refresh policy; the aggregate batch projection is never sent to owners, participating owners, public viewers, or realtime payloads.

Lifecycle eligibility is a coarse owner/GM projection, not control authority. It may contain Egg identity/revision, status, readiness/incubation disposition, fixed transfer/storage/facility/source-loss policies, closed blockers, and lifecycle-only transfer/hatch booleans. It must not contain source-loss observations, facility identity/evidence, offspring traits, parents, Breeder, Profiles, consent, commands, read sets, receipts, or hashes. Participating owners and public viewers receive no lifecycle-policy aggregate.

Accepted source loss is diagnostic only: current parent, Breeder, Project, or origin availability must never be used to enumerate private sheets to an owner or participant. Frozen snapshots remain server-private authority. Unknown facility evidence remains unavailable rather than becoming an observable campaign inventory oracle.

Fossil creation is current-GM-only. The submitted source, Paleontologist, Skills, Reanimation Machine, and Feature documents are evidence, never authority by declaration; the server reconstructs each from the exact current Trainer revision and campaign checkpoint. The source and machine use distinct inventory row IDs and quantity-backed unit ordinals. Species and every selectable trait resolve through deterministic command-bound server offers, and hatch-duration randomness is persisted before reduction. The accepted Egg freezes only audited source/provider hashes and bounded trait facts; it never reads documentary data or client labels.

The coarse fossil-creation projection contains only Egg identity, creation revision/status, Level, zero-parent count, and Restoration/Bond booleans. Source row, Species, Nature, Ability, inheritance, Edge instance, Skill ranks, machine custody, Feature instances, offer inventory, roll, read set, receipt, and hashes remain server-private. Trainer sheet events retain existing sheet-access descriptors, while Egg events are payload-free refreshes. Source/tool/provider loss after acceptance cannot become an owner-visible inventory oracle or hatch blocker.

A typed GM-source Egg freezes `gm-authored`, `mysterious`, `campaign-gift`, or `imported` provenance inside the private aggregate. Only a GM creation projection may identify that coarse class. Owner creation projections redact the class and import state; payload-free refreshes disclose neither. Imported source-system/record identities, record hashes, receipt hashes, the creating GM Profile, offers, read sets, receipts, and rolls remain server-private. The server rebuilds import evidence synchronously; raw imported content and client provenance never become authority. A BR-064 gift preserves this private origin byte-for-byte instead of relabeling the source.

Egg gifting uses two durable positive records: one `source-gift` and one linked `recipient-acceptance`. Each is bound to the current Egg revision, exact source and destination Trainer documents, the consenting Profile/control evidence, campaign minute, and a common expiry of at most 43,200 campaign minutes. Expiry is effective at equality. GM authority may execute an agreement but cannot manufacture, replace, or extend either consent. Stale Profile links, changed Trainer revisions, changed Egg ownership/revision, malformed records, asynchronous resolvers, extra consent, or mismatched counterpart IDs fail before private mechanics are read or written.

Invitation projection is a dedicated coarse shape for exactly the source owner or recipient. It contains only audience, opaque offer consent ID, Egg ID/revision, offered/accepted/transferred/revoked/expired state, counterparty Trainer slug, bounded actions, expiry, and generated campaign minute. It excludes Profile IDs, control evidence, source provenance, parent/Breeder facts, offspring blueprint, provider traits, commands, read sets, receipts, dependency hashes, and rolls. Settlement consumes both records and updates the Egg in one transaction. Realtime carries only bounded post-commit refresh material for diagnostic, GM, former-owner, new-owner, and public audiences; no consent or Egg payload is broadcast. A former owner's refresh removes access rather than revealing the recipient's private Egg state.

Hatch offers are current owner/GM capability projections, not mutation authority. The server derives box/team availability from the authoritative Egg and owner Trainer revision, requires current owner Profile control or current GM authentication, and regenerates the command-bound offer before consuming its exact declaration token. Team fullness reveals only a remaining-slot count and safe blocker; active and boxed Pokémon identities never leave the Trainer fact boundary. The projection excludes command, Egg, Trainer, actor/control, reference, security, option, and authority hashes except the opaque generic offer hash required for exact confirmation. Participating owners, foreign destination Trainers, stale offers, and client-authored roster counts receive no authority or Egg facts.

Hatch-special randomness is server-only and durable before reduction. One Egg receives exactly one command-bound d100; retries and recovery must reuse it. Totals 1 and 100 open a GM workflow but never automatically mean Shiny. A provider handoff without exact persisted use consumption cannot force the workflow, and an unreviewed configured table grants nothing. The bounded GM offer has three nonmechanical classifications and no free-text or Shiny option. Current GM authentication is required again for settlement; a client option ID, old offer, or editable outcome cannot confer authority.

The owner hatch-special projection reveals only coarse normal/pending/resolved state, whether a GM decision is required, and the closed resolved outcome. It omits roll identity/total/triggers and all offer/adjudication facts. The GM projection may add those bounded audit facts and safe presentation IDs, but it still omits commands, read sets, receipts, hashes, provider payloads, Trainer rosters, Profiles/control/consent, blueprint, parents, Breeder, and lineage. Realtime remains a four-audience refresh signal with no ledger payload and no participating-owner access.

Child-sheet construction is a server-private mechanics plan, not a preview projection. The client cannot supply nickname, Species labels, Level, Experience, Gender, Nature, Ability, Moves, HP, Shiny, Baby Template state, folder, or slug. Frozen IDs are resolved only through app-owned JSON and exact current compiled authority. The generated document intentionally omits Egg, origin, lineage, parent, Breeder, roll, adjudication, command, read-set, receipt, hash, and provider facts; those remain separate restricted aggregates. Compatibility Egg Move rows convey no lineage authority.

A blank row, second save, browser patch, map spawn, or client-retained plan cannot establish a child. BR-056 exposes no projection or mutation. BR-067 Baby Template authority is a self-hashed private Egg-derived record restored before calculation; editable `babyTemplate`, projected mechanics, forged `serverPrivate`, or an offer carrying only the right label cannot create or reactivate it. Starting-Level inheritance at Level 20 or higher is now resolved before child insertion with one durable record per reached checkpoint and the same typed provenance used after hatch.

Optional Baby Template choices require both the exact server evidence ID and a value hash over the reviewed runtime policy, canonical decline/application value, and frozen campaign-option snapshot. Ordinary Project, fossil, GM, and Playing God source paths issue only bounded server values. Missing, stale, browser-authored, enriched, duplicate, or policy-mismatched offers fail before Egg creation. Recovery changes only derived child values; it never writes Species reference data. Marsupial action/protection rules require a server-authored Marsupial application and cannot be acquired by an optional campaign template. Hatch completion is a server-authoritative multi-aggregate commit. The client cannot select the slug, mutate the plan, change the accepted destination, claim capacity, claim Species history, manufacture lineage, or choose write order. The current Egg, Trainer, clock, begin command, references, acquisition state, and allocation contract are rebuilt and compared to one complete read set before any phase-2 write. The child-plan hash is retained in the authorization receipt. Original owner control cannot be replaced by GM authority, and terminal retry additionally requires current control of the unchanged Egg owner Trainer.

Child sheet, Trainer link/reward, settled Egg, immutable origin, terminal result, and refresh rows either commit together or remain absent. This closes duplicate-child, duplicate-reward, orphan-lineage, roster-only, Egg-only, and pre-commit-publication races. Lineage mutation requires a caller-owned transaction and unique Egg and child identities. Exact terminal retry executes no reducer and is publication-silent.

Species history is a server-private immutable fact keyed by Trainer and canonical Species, not by current roster or Experience totals. An absent transactional read authorizes one hatch-source insert and one Experience point; a present read authorizes neither and must preserve the original source evidence. The validator compares the service outcome, Trainer revision/Experience arithmetic, source Egg, operation, campaign minute, and storage timestamp before any later hatch write. A forged history-presence claim, changed first fact, duplicate reward, or contradictory repository participant aborts phase 2. Reward outcome and history payload never enter the hatch projection or realtime rows.

BR-069 applies that boundary to capture, evolution, trade, reviewed migration, and GM review without making their private evidence a breeding command. Each external event has a deterministic self-hashed operation identity and a separate private terminal settlement unique by logical source event. Capture authority is retained by the accepted live-play operation; evolution and trade authority is the exact server-observed setup-save transition and unique current ownership; reviewed sources bind source-artifact and reviewer-authority hashes. Migration and GM review expose no browser route. Missing, duplicate, stale, asynchronous, enriched, malformed, or hash-drifted authority fails before reward or publication.

Release is deliberately a no-write history event. Neither roster removal nor deletion can reveal whether a Trainer has prior history, and reacquisition cannot award again. Source settlement, first facts, reward outcome, review IDs, Profile identities, Pokémon ownership scans, operation hashes, and acquisition definitions are campaign-private. Realtime carries only the existing authorized sheet refreshes; no acquisition payload is emitted. A source settlement's composite foreign key protects history retention, while exact retries append neither event rows nor publication.

The completion projection reveals only coarse settled navigation facts. It never reveals the child Species or traits, Trainer roster identities, first-acquisition outcome, blueprint, lineage content, parents, Breeder, consent, Profile/control evidence, command, read set, receipt, dependency/provider data, hashes, rolls, or adjudication. Full child and Trainer updates travel only through existing sheet-access descriptors; Egg refreshes are separate owner/GM rows with no participating-owner audience. Ledger, lineage, and receipt payloads are never realtime data.

Duplicate and concurrent commands confer no additional authority. Independent connections serialize at the database boundary and compare the same Egg, Trainer, acquisition, clock, and operation evidence before writes. A stale command cannot use a different operation ID to create a second child; a previously pending loser may only settle stale after current actor revalidation. Failure after any intermediate write rolls back the entire phase-2 prefix, including sequence allocation. Restart resumes persisted evidence rather than trusting client recovery claims.

Realtime retention is not mechanics recovery. A gap response carries cursor metadata only and causes complete snapshot replacement; it does not include pruned sheet, Egg, lineage, reward, or ledger data. Exact operation retry cannot republish or reconstruct deleted event rows. Publication failures retain durable access-described rows and accepted state, so an observer outage cannot become a reroll or rollback oracle.

Control never exceeds visibility. A deep link may adopt only an identity present in the current authorized projection. Unknown or contradictory projection data is discarded and refreshed from the server.

## Realtime and local persistence

Realtime events are refresh signals containing only bounded schema, sequence, aggregate-kind, hashed aggregate identity, revision, operation-kind, and audience-refresh scope fields. They do not carry parents, traits, rolls, consent payloads, commands, choices, sheets, lineage, notes, or authority.

Local persistence is limited to Workshop presentation preferences. Project, Egg, parent, consent, command, choice, roll, time, or authority payloads must not be stored in local storage or IndexedDB.

## Primary threats

The closed threat register covers:

- IDOR and existence-oracle attacks;
- cross-owner use without consent;
- hidden parent, Move, provider, or profile leakage;
- client mechanics and patch injection;
- rerolls, replay, and operation-ID collisions;
- stale consent and parent time-of-check/time-of-use races;
- concurrent double hatch and duplicate rewards;
- realtime and export leakage;
- restore tampering and missing rulesets;
- legacy map-metadata or editable-sheet authority confusion;
- oversized payload, enumeration, and write-rate exhaustion;
- parent evolution, trade, rename, or deletion after Egg acceptance.

Mitigations are exact command parsing, complete transactional read sets, expected revisions, persisted randomness, immutable snapshots, one Egg repository, operation hashes, exact retry results, audience-specific schemas, bounded payloads, rate limits, digest-bound exports, and restore validation.

Parent/source change evidence has `clientAuthority: none` and must encode exactly one reviewed semantic delta over storage-owned slug, revision, owner, folder, normalized document hash, Species identity, and reference-snapshot hash. Pre-check refresh is limited to same-identity evolution, folder movement, or retraining through the audited interruption path. Rename, trade, deletion, reference reinterpretation, and every post-check change fail closed until cancellation/new setup or a reviewed migration. Accepted Eggs never dereference live parents for mechanics: continuity loss is diagnostic only and cannot expose the new owner/location, alter private snapshots, pause incubation, remove readiness, or block hatch.

## Closed limits

The v1 policy bounds commands to 32 KiB, realtime events to 4 KiB, exports to 64 MiB, IDs to 160 characters, narrative fields to 500 characters, choices to 64 options, effective parent Moves to 64 each, inheritance candidates to 256, and operation rolls to 32. List pages default to 25 and cap at 100.

A breeding project has exactly two parents, at most two consent records, and a parent may participate in only one active project at a time. Player writes are limited to 30 per minute and GM writes to 120 per session per minute. Exact-retry receipts remain with campaign data and are not expired merely to permit rerolling.

Negative, unsafe, duplicate, over-bound, and unknown-field input is rejected.

### BR-084 executable certification

`data/breeding-automation/security-certification.json` binds all five audiences, 22 privacy fields, seven Workshop API surfaces, 13 reviewed threats, 22 abuse controls, and the authorization, consent, information-flow, malformed-input, and audit matrices to current runtime paths and focused assertions. It is an acceptance report, not a new authorization or mechanics source.

Every Breeding POST reads at most 32 KiB of valid UTF-8 JSON before its closed request parser runs. Declared and observed byte overflow returns a bounded 413 without echoing input. Confirmed Project creation, non-inspection hatch actions, and non-view consent actions then pass through request admission: 30 attempted writes per selected player Profile per fixed minute and a conservative 120-write authenticated GM process-session bucket. Exhaustion returns only 429 plus `Retry-After`; it cannot advance campaign time, alter an operation, consume an option, redraw randomness, or change exact-retry evidence. Clock regression and rate-key exhaustion fail closed, and expired buckets are pruned within a fixed 4,096-key memory bound.

The current authentication boundary exposes no client-safe unique GM session identifier, so the GM bucket is deliberately process-wide and stricter than a per-session allowance. A browser header, cookie payload, Profile selector, or aggregate ID never chooses a rate key. Repository list defaults and maxima are 25 and 100, and active Project insertion atomically rejects a parent already present in another active Project.

## Audit and failure behavior

Mutations record operation and command hashes, actor and role, aggregate revisions, authorization and consent evidence, ruleset/source/provider hashes, roll and choice identities, GM override reason IDs, terminal results, and event identities.

Incubation accepts elapsed time only from the authoritative campaign clock and one complete read set. The accepted Egg target is frozen, progress is clamped, pause/resume is explicit, and every interval has one immutable command-bound segment. Parent, Breeder, source, storage, map, encounter, browser, process, and wall-clock changes cannot implicitly pause or credit time. BR-062 permits only the exact continuous Egg Warmer item 2:1 contribution; every unknown, stale, malformed, duplicated, or extraneous modifier remains unauthorized.

Modifier handoffs are server-private evidence, not declarations. Ability and Capability authority must be current, effective, unsuppressed, unambiguous, and synchronously resolved. Item authority requires exact owner inventory-row and quantity-backed unit custody. Serpent’s Mark evidence is frozen on the Egg and child evidence is retained only in `serverPrivate`, which is removed from player projections and preserved against player saves. Egg Warmer Capability cooldown and randomness are campaign-time and persisted-ledger facts; a client roll, sheet label, wall clock, or handoff by itself cannot consume or activate the resource. BR-065 may execute only the exact fossil Restoration/Bond and Reanimation Machine policies described above. BR-067 alone may execute Baby Template, Marsupial pouch, Parental Bond hatch interaction, and Playing God artificial-Egg mechanics. BR-068 alone may execute post-hatch inheritance learning, after rebuilding the terminal Egg, exact child, unique owner link, hydrated origin prefix, campaign clock, current references, active offers, read set, and owner or targeted GM authority. Clients can submit only server-issued option IDs; canonical Moves, replacement rows, prerequisite outcomes, lineage records, and provenance are wire-forbidden. Whole-sheet GM and player saves preserve an existing server-authored permanent source only for its unique surviving Move-name row and strip every submitted new or rewritten source.
